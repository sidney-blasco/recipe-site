Cakes, number fourteen

Devil's Food Cake

Two dark layers, a cocoa buttercream, and no apologies.

Makes
one 9-inch layer cake
Hands on
40 minutes
Start to finish
2½ hours

Every family has a chocolate cake that settles arguments. This is ours. It owes its depth to hot coffee poured straight into the batter, which wakes the cocoa up and keeps the crumb tender for days, if it lasts that long.

Bake it the night before if you can. The flavor deepens overnight, and the layers are easier to frost once they've had time to firm.

For the cake

  • 2 cupsall-purpose flour
  • ¾ cupDutch-process cocoa
  • 2 cupssugar
  • 1 cuphot coffee
  • 2large eggs, at room temperature

Coffee! Don't skip it. Nobody will taste it, everybody will notice.
